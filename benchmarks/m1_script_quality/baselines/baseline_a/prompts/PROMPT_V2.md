# PROMPT V2

First, **UNDERSTAND WHAT HAPPENED** by internally reading and comprehending the complete supplied Japanese source text. 
Then, **DECIDE HOW TO TELL IT** by writing a storytelling script in Vietnamese based entirely on this source.

## CORE OBJECTIVE
- Output ONLY the final script. Do not output headings generated by the model, explanations, source summary, analysis, plan, quality score, or reasoning.

## STORYTELLING TRANSFORMATION
- Do NOT mechanically preserve source paragraph order.
- Identify the chapter's most curiosity-generating situation.
- Prioritize story beats by narrative importance.
- Compress repetitive exposition.
- Delay or merge background details when they slow momentum.
- Preserve chronology where necessary for comprehension.
- Allow a hook to preview a source-supported moment before briefly establishing context.
- Return naturally to the chapter's causal sequence.

## HOOK
- The first approximately 50–100 Vietnamese words should create curiosity quickly.
- The hook should come from material actually present in the supplied source. It may emphasize contrast, unusual situation, conflict, emotional question, irony, or unanswered mystery.
- Do NOT fabricate drama or reveal future events.
- The hook should make the viewer want to know: "Why is this happening?"

## EXPOSITION BUDGET
- Background exposition exists to support the active story.
- Do not spend several consecutive paragraphs describing appearance, reputation, personality, school status, or protagonist reasoning when those facts can be compressed.
- Prefer `1 strong detail + 1 meaningful contrast + move the story forward` rather than exhaustive descriptive preservation.

## INFORMATION PRIORITY
Prioritize information in this order:
1. Events that change the situation
2. Character decisions
3. Emotional reactions
4. Relationship-relevant moments
5. Setup/payoff or open loops
6. Only then decorative description

## NATURAL SPOKEN VIETNAMESE
- Target: spoken Vietnamese for a 15–25 YouTube audience.
- Prefer conversational sentence rhythm, clear vocabulary, natural phrasing, moderately short sentences, and expressive but not melodramatic narration.
- Avoid ornate, archaic, overly literary, or translation-like Vietnamese when a simple spoken expression conveys the same meaning.

## CREATOR COMMENTARY & HUMOR
- Allow light narrator personality (like a friend telling you an interesting romcom story, not a Wikipedia summary).
- Commentary must arise from the source situation. Do not invent facts, ridicule characters unfairly, become meme spam, or interrupt emotional scenes.
- Use light situational humor where the source naturally permits it (e.g., contradiction between what a character says and does, awkward social distance, ironic behavior, understated narrator reaction).
- Do NOT force jokes into serious/emotional moments or add modern memes unrelated to the source.

## ROMANCE & CHARACTER FIDELITY
- Keep romance proportional to source evidence. Do NOT declare attraction where none exists, imply a relationship already exists, invent sexual tension, or foreshadow specific future romance facts. The tone can suggest "this encounter may matter" only when the source itself supports such implication.
- Do not turn reserved characters into confident flirts, comedians, or melodramatic protagonists.
- Do not exaggerate emotional states beyond source evidence. Creative narration may change presentation, but not character truth.

## FACTUAL DISCIPLINE & SCENE STATE
- Every factual statement about past events, living conditions, relationships, motives, character traits, or scene state must be supported by the supplied source or clearly framed as narrator interpretation rather than fact.
- Maintain internal consistency of scene state: who is present, where they are, sitting/standing/moving, possession of objects, and sequence of actions.

## OUTPUT LANGUAGE INTEGRITY & ENDING
- Output must contain natural Vietnamese prose. Names and established Japanese honorifics may remain. Avoid accidental fragments from unrelated writing systems/languages.
- The ending should preserve meaningful payoff, irony, open loop, or unresolved tension when present in the source.
- Do NOT append generic YouTube CTA (like, subscribe, comment, see you next video).

## TARGET LENGTH
- Target length: Approximately 900–1100 Vietnamese words. (Preferred ~1000 words).
