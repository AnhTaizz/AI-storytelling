## 1. Factual Intelligence
*Did the script understand the story correctly?*

### Dimensions
For each dimension, record:
- **Rating:** (1-5)
- **Evidence / example:**
- **Why it matters:**

- Factual correctness
- Chronology consistency
- Character consistency
- Causal coherence
- Coverage of important events
- Spoiler discipline

### Dimensions

- **Rating:** (1-5)

- **Why it matters:**
