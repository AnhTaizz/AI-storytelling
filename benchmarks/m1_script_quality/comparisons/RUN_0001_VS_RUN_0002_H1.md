# H1 Comparative Review

## Experiment
Comparing `RUN_0001_BASELINE_A` (PROMPT_V1) against `RUN_0002_H1_PROMPT_V2` (PROMPT_V2).

## Research Question
**H1 — Stronger Narrative Instructions**: Can stronger narrative instructions improve creator-oriented storytelling quality without adding architectural complexity?

## Controlled Variables
- **Source**: Chapter 1 (`chapter_001.txt`)
- **Source Language**: Japanese
- **Output Language**: Vietnamese
- **Model/Runtime**: Gemini 3.1 Pro (High)
- **Architecture**: Direct source-to-script
- **Target Audience**: 15-25
- **Approximate Length**: 900–1100 words

Primary changed variable: `PROMPT_V1 → PROMPT_V2`

## Methodology Limitations
Both used the agent runtime. Strict machine-enforced context isolation was not independently verified.

## Mechanical Comparison
- **RUN_0001 Word Count**: 1125 (Target: 900–1100)
- **RUN_0002 Word Count**: 1045 (Target: 900–1100)
- **RUN_0001 Target Met**: false
- **RUN_0002 Target Met**: true
- **RUN_0001 Contamination**: true (Bengali)
- **RUN_0002 Contamination**: true (Cyrillic)

RUN_0002 improved compliance with the provisional output-length target. However, length compliance does not independently mean pacing improved, as length and pacing are distinct.

## Factual Intelligence Comparison
### Unsupported Inventions (F001)
RUN_0002 introduces new expressive details that drift from the source text:
- Everyone at school worships Mahiru ("tôn thờ")
- Mahiru is a true genius ("thiên tài thực sự")
- The rain is cutting cold ("cái lạnh cắt da cắt thịt")
- Her face looks as though the whole world collapsed ("như thể cả thế giới đã sụp đổ")
- Assumptions that go materially beyond Chapter 1 ("khởi đầu cho một câu chuyện dài phía trước")

These represent creative exaggerations or unsupported factual assertions, demonstrating that stronger storytelling instructions can inadvertently increase factual drift.

### Scene State Inconsistency (F002)
RUN_0001 had a sitting/standing inconsistency.
RUN_0002 maintains Mahiru coherently seated on the swing throughout the scene.
**IMPROVED / RESOLVED IN THIS RUN**

### Hair Color Fidelity
Japanese source: `亜麻色` (flaxen)
RUN_0001: `màu hạt lanh` (flax)
RUN_0002: `màu hạt dẻ` (chestnut)
RUN_0002 introduces a source fidelity regression regarding Mahiru's hair color, likely prioritizing a more natural-sounding Vietnamese descriptor at the expense of strict accuracy.

## Narrative Intelligence Comparison
### Exposition / Pacing (F004)
RUN_0001 begins with dense character and background exposition before reaching the rain scene.
RUN_0002 effectively uses a rain hook, rewinds to backfill context, and returns to the inciting situation. This dramatically reduces exposition density and improves the time to the inciting situation.
**IMPROVED**

### Source-Order Retelling (F005)
RUN_0001 directly mimics the chronological source sequence.
RUN_0002 restructures the narrative flow intentionally (hook -> rewind -> present).
This provides evidence that a single-step LLM can perform at least some narrative restructuring without a dedicated Narrative Planner, though it is not yet justified solely by source-order failure.
**IMPROVED**

### Style Match (F006)
RUN_0002 successfully reduces the ornate literary phrasing and stiff translation tone present in RUN_0001. However, it introduces over-casual phrasing and anime/meta commentary ("giống hệt mấy bộ anime rom-com kinh điển"), which may not entirely fit the intended channel voice.
**IMPROVED BUT IMPERFECT**

### Humor / Commentary (F007)
RUN_0002 successfully introduces narrator commentary, playful reactions, and a conversational style, improving engagement. However, this increases the risk of exaggeration.
**IMPROVED**

### Ending Comparison
RUN_0001 accurately reflects the subtle source ending ("Ít nhất là vào lúc đó.").
RUN_0002 adds a stronger interpretation ("khởi đầu cho một câu chuyện dài phía trước").
- **Narrative impact**: improved (stronger dramatic closure).
- **Spoiler/fidelity discipline**: slightly worse (over-interprets the source and implies future events).

## New Regressions
- **Hair Color Fidelity**: "hạt dẻ" instead of "hạt lanh".
- **ENTERTAINMENT_FIDELITY_TRADEOFF**: Increasing the hook, commentary, emotional emphasis, and conversational style inherently increases exaggeration, unsupported interpretation, future implication, and factual drift.

## What H1 Improved
- Pacing and exposition prioritization
- Narrative structure (breaking strict chronological retelling)
- Stylistic conversational tone and humor
- Mechanical output word count compliance

## What H1 Did Not Solve
- Factual drift and unsupported inventions
- Output language corruption (Cyrillic token contamination persists)

## Trade-offs
**ENTERTAINMENT_FIDELITY_TRADEOFF**: The push for stronger narrative presentation (better hook, pacing, and narrator commentary) comes at the direct cost of factual fidelity and an increased risk of over-interpreting or spoiling future implications.

## H1 Verdict
**PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED**
Strong narrative prompting is a valuable low-cost improvement and should remain part of the system, but it cannot by itself reliably enforce factual truth and output integrity.

## Architecture Implications
- **H2 (Separate Story Brief)**: The `ENTERTAINMENT_FIDELITY_TRADEOFF` suggests separating factual representation from creative narration. RUN_0001 was factually stable but narratively weak; RUN_0002 is narratively strong but factually loose. A dedicated Story Brief stage separating "what is true" from "how to tell it" is justified for exploration.
- **H5 (Output Validator)**: Language contamination occurred in both runs (Bengali in RUN_0001, Cyrillic in RUN_0002). This provides strong empirical motivation for an Output Validator to guarantee clean target-language output.
- **Advanced RAG / Graph**: Not justified. Both experiments operate on a single clean-start chapter, and the observed failures relate to storytelling style, factual discipline within the provided text, and output token integrity. Long-range memory has not yet been tested.

## Recommended Next Hypothesis
Design a controlled **H2 Story Brief** experiment to separate factual understanding from creative storytelling.
